# Demo script

Lead with a real store. The fixture injection is a second beat, labeled as a safety demo.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Landing | “I need a price from a real store, and I do not want the browser to order anything.” |
| 0:15–0:35 | Compare page, example “HP 26A toner”, Quill and CDW checked | Click **Compare these stores**. |
| 0:35–1:20 | Screenshots and the price each page actually showed | If a card says blocked or unverified, say that. Do not read out a number that is not on the card. |
| 1:20–1:40 | Request a quote, show the draft, Deny or Approve | “Approve only saves the draft. Nothing is sent.” |
| 1:40–2:20 | Safety demo | Switch to the fictional shops. Show QuickQuote’s injection, the blocked call, and Deny. |
| 2:20–2:40 | Audit hashes | “These match the screenshots.” |

Do not claim a freight-inclusive total unless the page showed freight. Do not claim a vision check unless the card says verified. Do not open the landing on fixture prices. The safety demo is the second beat in the table above.
